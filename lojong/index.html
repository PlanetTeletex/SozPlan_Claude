The seven points of mind training were set down by Chekawa Yeshe Dorje (1102–1176), drawing on the instructions Atisha brought from Suvarnadvipa to Tibet. The slogans appear here in their familiar English wording; the short commentary beneath each one is a plain practice note, not a translation of any classical commentary.
